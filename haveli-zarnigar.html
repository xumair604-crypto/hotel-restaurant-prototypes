Welcome to Haveli Zarnigar

Your home in the heart of old Lahore

Built by a family of spice traders in 1893 and restored brick by brick, Haveli Zarnigar keeps its painted ceilings, wooden jharokhas and inner courtyard. Our team has hosted travellers, families and history lovers for nearly two decades, and we know every lane of the Walled City.

Stay with us for warm Lahori hospitality, modern comforts and easy access to the Fort, the Mosque and Food Street, all within walking distance.

Around the Haveli

The Walled City on your doorstep

Step out of our carved wooden gate and you are among the thirteen historic gates of Lahore. Brass workers, kite makers and chai stalls fill the lanes. The concierge desk books guided walks, tonga rides through the old bazaars and evening qawwali at the shrines.

  • Wazir Khan Mosque10 min walk
  • Fort Road Food Street5 min walk

Welcome to Roshandan

Real Lahori food, cooked the slow way

Roshandan means the small skylight in an old Lahori house, the window that lets in light and lets out the smoke of the kitchen. We opened on Fort Road in 2014 with one tandoor and a family recipe for karahi. Today our kitchen serves families, food lovers and travellers from around the world.

Every dish is made fresh: spices ground in-house every morning, meat marinated overnight, bread baked to order. Nothing frozen, nothing rushed.

Inside Our Kitchen

How a karahi reaches your table

The same four steps every day, from the morning spice grind to the iron wok at your table.

  1. 6:00 am

    Grind the masala

    Whole coriander, cumin and red chilli are roasted and stone-ground fresh each morning.

  2. 12 hours

    Marinate overnight

    Mutton and chicken rest in yogurt, ginger and garlic so the flavour goes all the way through.

  3. 15–20 min

    Fire the wok

    Cooked to order over a high flame with tomato, green chilli and desi ghee. Never pre-made.

  4. At your table

    Serve it sizzling

    Brought out in the karahi it was cooked in, with julienned ginger and naan from the tandoor.
